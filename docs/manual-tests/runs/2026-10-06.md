## Run A — iPhone / iOS Safari

**Device:** iPhone 14 or newer, iOS 17+, Safari.

### Sheet & viewport
- [x] ✅ **A1 Sheet bottom.** On a church card at every snap, the sheet's bottom corners are square and run under the search bar — no rounded gap, nothing under the home indicator. Scroll so the address bar collapses: no jump.
- [x] ✅ **A2 Sheet drag.** Drag the sheet by the **body** of a card — a short card with nothing to scroll, then a long one: scroll it, close, open a short one, drag again. Never stuck header-only.
- [x] ✅ **A3 Scroll chaining.** At the top snap, scroll the card to its end — the bounce stays in the sheet, the map doesn't move.
- [x] ✅ **A4 Panning.** Pan and pinch the map around a dense area (Paris, Lyon) with the list open. Feels fluid, no stutter as the list refreshes; tapping a tile afterwards opens that church.
- [x] ✅ **A5 Server-rendered pages.** Cold-open `/diocese/lyon` and `/ville/marseille` in a fresh tab. The list appears, then becomes interactive without the sheet jumping or the list flashing; the sheet drags normally. _List narrows to the map viewport ~1 s after hydration (Lyon 31 → 0, Marseille 7 → 3) — by design._

### Community feedback
- [x] ✅ **A6 Opening a panel.** On a church card, tap *Compléter*. The sheet expands and the panel's bottom scrolls into view; **no keyboard pops up**. Tap the pill again — the panel closes.
- [x] ✅ **A7 Snap points after the panel.** After A6, drag the sheet down and back up — the snap points are where they were, not shifted up.
- [x] ❌ **A8 Scroll from a textarea.** With a panel open, start a scroll with your finger **on** the textarea and drag. The keyboard does **not** open on release. Then tap the textarea — it focuses and stays visible above the keyboard. _Scroll-start OK (keyboard stays closed). On focus the textarea scrolls under the card's sticky header — same bug as Android B3._
- [x] ✅ **A9 Photo.** In *Compléter*, add a photo: the iOS picker opens (library and camera), the preview shows, sending succeeds. _Picker + preview OK; send N/A — staging writes to the prod API._
- [x] ✅ **A10 Reply.** On a church with community comments, tap *Répondre* under one. The inline field stays above the keyboard while typing; send it — the reply appears under that comment and the keyboard dismisses cleanly. _Field stays above the keyboard; send N/A — staging writes to the prod API._

### Install & share
- [x] ✅ **A11 Home screen.** Share → *Sur l'écran d'accueil*. The icon is the white logo on deepblue and opens full-screen (no Safari bars), with nothing under the notch.
- [x] ✅ **A12 Share.** In the installed app, *Partager* on a church card opens the iOS share sheet; the shared link is the clean `/church/<uuid>`.

### Carried over
- [x] ✅ **A13 Rotation.** With a church card open: landscape → portrait. The sheet/modal is still there and correct. _Fixed since 2026-09-05._
- [x] ❌ **A14 Search bar.** Tap the search input and type one character — the pill keeps its height and alignment. Tap the clear (X) — the keyboard stays. _Pill thins (44 → 33.5 px) once autocomplete results appear — same bug as Android B6; not re-tested step by step._
